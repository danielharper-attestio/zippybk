What lenders typically look at

  • Time in business (often 6+ months)
  • Monthly or annual revenue
  • Owner’s personal credit
  • Industry and existing debt
Documents to have ready

Recent business bank statements, a government-issued ID, and your business tax ID (EIN). Some products also ask for tax returns or financial statements.
