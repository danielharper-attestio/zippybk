What to do after an accident

  • Get medical care right away. Some injuries don’t show symptoms for days, and medical records document your injuries.
  • Report the accident. Call the police and get a copy of the report number.
  • Document the scene. Take photos of vehicles, injuries, road conditions and get witness contact info.
  • Be careful what you say. Don’t admit fault or give a recorded statement to the other driver’s insurer.
  • Keep every record. Medical bills, missed-work notes and repair estimates all support your claim.
  • Talk to a lawyer before you settle. Early settlement offers are often lower than what a claim may be worth.

Injury case questions

How much does it cost to hire you?

Nothing upfront. Attorney fees are a percentage of any recovery, and you pay no attorney fee if there’s no recovery. Case costs are explained in the fee agreement.

How long do I have to file a claim?

Each state sets a deadline, called a statute of limitations, and it can be as short as one year for some claims. Contact a lawyer as soon as possible.

What is my case worth?

It depends on your injuries, medical costs, lost income, insurance limits and who was at fault. A case review is the best way to get an estimate.
