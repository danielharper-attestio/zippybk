Demo 4 — Sent by the page's own code

Some sites never submit a form at all: a "Send" button runs the site's own script, which posts the answers to its server and swaps the form for a thank-you message on the same page. There is no submit for a tracker to see — ours still notices the send, keeps a copy of the answers taken at the click, and makes the lead once the page shows it went through.

By clicking Send you agree that we may contact you by phone and text message, including by autodialer, at the number provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase.
