Demo 2 — The page says no

This page checks the answers itself, like many client sites do: the phone number must have 10 digits.

  1. Fill the form with a phone number of only 9 digits (e.g. 512555014) and press Submit. The page shows an error and stays.
  2. Watch the status box: about 5 seconds later the tracker reports that the page turned the submit down — so it is not a lead. It appears only under Failed submissions, as "Not submitted".
  3. Fix the phone number and submit again: now the page accepts it, and it becomes a lead with the same record ID.

A broken email (like john@example) is turned down too — by our server's own email check, even before the page's answer matters.
