Record-at-submit demo (local)dashboard: localhost:3016

Try the new record flow

Each demo is a small form with our tracker on it, like a client's page. Every new switch is on for the two local test accounts. After you submit, the thank-you page asks Inspect for the record — exactly what a client's server does — and shows what the record holds.

1. Saved at submitThe record ID comes from our server; the form waits (at most 2 s) until the record is saved, then goes. Inspect finds it at once. 2. The page says noThe page rejects the submit (a short phone number): no lead. Fix it and submit again: a lead. 3. Two steps + a Facebook-style wipeThe browser loses everything between the steps; step 2 still joins step 1's record. (Opens on its own address — see below.) 4. Sent by the page's own codeNo normal submit at all — the page sends the answers itself. It's still caught.

Good to know

  • The ID in the dark bar at the top is the record ID our tracker put on the page's form (the hidden field a client receives with the lead).
  • Demos 1, 2 and 4 use the dev@test.com account; demo 3 uses dev2@test.com. You can open each record in the dashboard after logging in as that account.
  • Demo 3 runs on its own address, 127.0.0.1:8795: a browser keeps each address's stored data apart, so demo 3 never continues a visit you left unfinished in demos 1, 2 or 4 (they belong to the other test account — on a real client site every page is one account, so this can't happen there).
  • Demo 3 joins step 2 to step 1 only when this browser has one journey on the account in the last 15 minutes (a safety rule: we never guess between two). To repeat it, wait 15 minutes or use another browser.
  • Use any made-up name and email, but not monitoring@test.com (that's the account's monitoring address, which is never a lead).
